35:## Call locations
37:- A call location is the beginning of the complete call expression.
38:- All syntactic call expressions are recorded, including nested calls.
39:- An immediately invoked result is a separate call expression.
40:- `require(...)` is both a CommonJS import and a call expression.
41:- `import(...)` is both a dynamic-import observation and a call expression.
42:- For `object.property(...)`, `receiver` is the object and `callee` is the
44:- For a simple `name(...)` call, `receiver` is null and `callee` is the name.
45:- For a computed member call such as `_[method](...)`, the receiver is known
46:  but the property is unresolved.
47:- Calls through the result of another call are retained with a note explaining
48:  that the callee is an immediately invoked or chained result.
56:- Anonymous callback functions are not added to the named-functions list.
